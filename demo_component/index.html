OPERATING GUIDE

Help & procedures

Handle a complaint

  1. Enter the original message, order reference and known customer details.
  2. Check the category, proposed team and source quotes.
  3. Open the case in Review queue. Correct the draft and record your decision.
  4. Approve only after checking the source and proposed next steps.
  5. Preview the customer email or department handoff, check the recipient and message, and explicitly confirm sending. You can also copy an approved reply for manual use.

Possible safety incidents

Read every complaint for risk, including cases not flagged by the service. A possible incident must stay urgent and routed to Product Safety. Record the escalation in the review note and contact the responsible colleague using your established incident procedure.

Uncertain results

Ask for clarification rather than guessing. A review note is required for uncertain cases, safety escalations and changes. Automated checks can miss risks or misunderstand wording.

Investigate recurring issues

Open Complaint insights and inspect the supporting messages. Courier order shares are configured assumptions, not shipment-level damage rates. Obtain shipment volumes and product mix before attributing a cause.

Service unavailable

Check Service settings. If OpenAI fails, the error remains visible. Enable rules fallback only if you accept a less flexible suggestion that requires careful review. No complaint is automatically approved.

Data and access

Keep this local installation on a trusted device. Staff login, inbox synchronisation and refund processing are not configured. Email sending requires a separately configured SMTP connection. Consult the handover guide before connecting operational customer data.

Training data

The optional training dataset contains fictional customers, couriers and deliberately generated patterns. Use it for familiarisation, not business decisions.
